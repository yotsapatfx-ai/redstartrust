# Offerpad Leaves NYSE for Nasdaq on 31 August, Keeping the OPAD Ticker

Same stock, same four letters, new venue. Nasdaq has told the market that **Offerpad Solutions Inc.** is expected to begin trading on the **Nasdaq Capital Market** on **31 August 2026**, after its final session on the **NYSE** on 28 August.

Word of it came not as a corporate press release but as a technical alert to traders and to the firms that carry market data — where the practical work of moving a listing happens.

## What the notice says

Nasdaq set out the transfer in **Data Technical News #2026-18**, issued on **Thursday 27 August 2026**. Its wording is deliberately measured: on 08/31/2026, Offerpad Solutions Inc. (NYSE: OPAD) **is expected to be listed** and begin trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the same ticker symbol OPAD.

That hedge is worth keeping. The notice describes an expectation rather than a finished event, and it gives no reason for the move.

## What does not change for holders

The ticker is the detail most shareholders will look for first, and it is unchanged: **OPAD stays OPAD** on the new venue.

So does the **CUSIP, 67623L505** — the identifying code assigned to a security, the number that back-office systems use to match a trade to the right instrument. The **round lot** is unchanged as well, at **100 shares**, meaning the standard order size the market quotes and trades in stays exactly where it was. Nothing in the notice asks a shareholder to do anything.

## The transfer timeline

Three dates run back to back with no gap between venues. Offerpad trades on the **NYSE through 28 August 2026**. **After the close on that day**, redistributors of market data are instructed to update their databases to reflect the new listing market. Trading on the **Nasdaq Capital Market is expected to begin on 31 August 2026**.

## The data plumbing behind it

The quote stream moves with the listing. From **pre-market hours on 31 August 2026**, bids, offers and completed trades in OPAD appear on the **UTP Quotation Data Feed (UQDF)** and the **UTP Trade Data Feed (UTDF)** — the feeds that carry quotes and trades for Nasdaq-listed securities.

Nasdaq also tells redistributors to **maintain historical data for OPAD prior to the transfer date**, so the record on either side of the switch stays continuous rather than restarting on the new market. For questions, the notice points to Nasdaq Trading Services and Nasdaq Data Client Success, and lists **Benjamin Lawrence**, Associate Director of Exchange Transfers at Nasdaq, and **Cortney Read**, VP Investor Relations and Communications at Offerpad Solutions Inc., as contacts.

Until the first quote crosses the feed on 31 August, the exchange's own word for all of it remains "expected".
