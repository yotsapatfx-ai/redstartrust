# CICC Moves to Absorb Dongxing and Cinda in a Share Swap Building a 1-Trillion-Yuan Brokerage

The deal that would create a brokerage holding **1 trillion yuan** in assets did not begin with a hunt for a rival. It began with a single shareholder that already owned all three firms.

## What the deal is, and how big

China International Capital Corp, or **CICC**, is moving to absorb two smaller brokers, **Dongxing Securities** and **Cinda Securities**, in a deal that would create a firm with about 1 trillion yuan in assets, the *South China Morning Post* reported on 20 November 2025.

The two reports convert that sum differently. SCMP put it at **US$140 billion**, while a Yicai report carried on the Shanghai Stock Exchange website and dated 16 June 2026 put it at **US$147.9 billion**.

Once combined, SCMP reported, the firm would rank **fourth** in China's brokerage industry, behind only Citic Securities, Guotai Haitong Securities and Huatai Securities.

## The mechanics of the swap

CICC is paying in its own stock rather than cash, a **share swap**. Under the draft acquisition plan set out in the 16 June 2026 report, CICC would issue **more than 3.1 billion mainland shares at 36.68 yuan, or US$5.42, each**.

The same report prices Dongxing at **16.05 yuan** and Cinda at **19.11 yuan** in the swap, with exchange ratios of **1 to 0.4376** and **1 to 0.5210** respectively. A swap ratio simply says how many new shares each old share becomes.

## One owner on every side of the table

Ownership is what separates this from an ordinary acquisition. **Central Huijin Investment** controls all three companies, the 16 June 2026 report said: as of 31 December it held **40 per cent** of CICC directly, **45 per cent** of Dongxing indirectly and **79 per cent** of Cinda indirectly. SCMP described Central Huijin as a unit of China's sovereign wealth fund.

The surviving company would take on the two targets' **assets, liabilities, businesses, employees, contracts, qualifications, rights and obligations**.

## The three firms on paper

The 16 June 2026 report also set out where each stands. It listed CICC's operating revenue at **28.5 billion yuan** and net profit at **9.8 billion yuan**, Dongxing's at **4.7 billion yuan** and **2.1 billion yuan**, and Cinda's at **4 billion yuan** and **1.9 billion yuan**.

Assets under management stretch the distance further. As of 31 December, CICC held **782.8 billion yuan**, against **114.2 billion yuan** at Dongxing and **130 billion yuan** at Cinda. On the day of that report, CICC traded **0.7 per cent** higher at 33.93 yuan in Shanghai, Dongxing **0.5 per cent** higher at 12.93 yuan and Cinda **0.1 per cent** higher at 15.48 yuan.

## Where the deal stands

The timeline starts on **19 November**, when CICC signed a preliminary share-swap agreement with both firms, with the detailed scheme released a month later. The **Shanghai Stock Exchange has accepted the application**, according to the report dated 16 June 2026, which cited a company statement.

Several layers of approval remain. SCMP reported that the exchange had approved a **trading suspension of up to 25 days**, and that the deal still needs sign-off from **company boards, shareholders and regulators**.

CICC said in that 20 November 2025 report: "The restructuring is conducive to building a first-class investment bank and supporting the reform of the financial market and the high-quality development of the securities industry."

For now, the 1-trillion-yuan figure is a target waiting on approvals, not a line on anyone's balance sheet.
