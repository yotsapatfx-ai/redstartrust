## 📊 Key Facts (from raw data — do not add figures)
- The notice is **Equity Trader Alert #2026-38: Nasdaq Trading Halt Rules Update**
- **Issued Wednesday 8 July 2026** · **effective 10 August 2026**
- Markets affected: **The Nasdaq Stock Market (Nasdaq) · Nasdaq Texas (NTX) · Nasdaq PSX**
- The exchanges are implementing **coordinated amendments** clarifying **three areas**
  1. **Regulatory Halt Authority** — the basis includes provisions from **SIP Plans**, discretionary halts and mandatory halts
  2. **Trading Resumption Procedures** — detailed rules on how **Primary Listing Markets** initiate and resume trading following regulatory halts, plus clarified obligations for other exchanges
  3. **Operational Halts** — clarifies the Nasdaq Exchanges' discretion to impose halts **affecting only their own market**
- The notice states the amendments promote *"consistent and transparent treatment of events that may affect multiple exchanges"* while maintaining exchange discretion in certain instances
- Four rule filings cited: **SR-NASDAQ-2022-017_Amendment_1 · SR-BX-2023-007 · SR-Phlx-2022-49 · Nasdaq UTP Amendment 50**
- **Saturday UAT production testing available 8 August 2026**

## 🪝 Hook
When a stock stops trading, the questions that follow are always the same: who called the halt, who decides it is over, and what happens on the other venues trading the same shares. Nasdaq has just written all three answers into a single set of rules.
