## 📊 Key Facts (from raw data — do not add figures)
- The **U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC)** issued a **request for comment** · **release number 9286-26** · dated **19 August 2026** · Washington
- Source headline: **CFTC Requests Comment on the Listing of Compute Derivatives Contracts**
- Purpose: **to improve the agency's understanding and oversight of derivatives markets in compute**
- **Quote — Chairman Michael S. Selig:** *"America cannot win the AI race without a robust derivatives market for compute."*
- He added that **American markets will establish standards for compute trading as they did for industrial commodities**, calling the request **"the first step toward establishing clear rules"**
- **Five topics for comment:** compute cash market size and liquidity · market oversight considerations · manipulation concerns · customer protection issues · **perpetual compute futures**
- The request **invites feedback on all aspects of compute markets**
- **Comment deadline: 60 days following Federal Register publication**
- **Related citation: 91 FR 54259**
