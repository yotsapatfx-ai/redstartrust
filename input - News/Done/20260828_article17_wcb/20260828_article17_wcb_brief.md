## 📊 Key Facts (from raw data — do not add figures)
- **CME Group said Thursday** (reported by LeapRate on **27 August 2026**) that it plans to launch **E-mini equity factor futures on 21 September**, **pending regulatory review**
- The move expands the exchange's equity suite with contracts offering **tailored exposure to S&P 500 and Dow Jones indexes**
- CME said equity factors are **performance indicators that can help investors capture growth, navigate volatility and diversify holdings**
- The contracts are expected to **complement single- and multi-factor strategies**, delivering **capital efficiencies for risk management and relative value trading**
- **The suite: E-mini S&P 500 growth, value, quality, momentum and low volatility futures**, alongside **E-mini Dow Jones US Dividend 100 futures**
- **Quote — Joseph Hickey, global head of equity products at CME Group:** *"Market participants are increasingly turning to equity factors to target specific components of risk and build more tailor-made portfolios."* and *"Our E-mini Equity Factor futures will provide additional flexibility for gaining and managing exposure to specific factors, with the centralized liquidity, margin offsets and seamless trading that clients rely on at CME Group."*
- **Robert Ross, chief commercial officer at S&P Dow Jones Indices**, said the launch supports a shared focus on delivering transparent and efficient index-based tools, giving market participants more precise ways to express views and manage risk — **the source paraphrases this; it is not a direct quotation**
- The contracts will be **eligible for margin offsets with other cleared equity products at CME Group** · **listed on and subject to the rules of CME** · available on **CME Globex** and through **privately negotiated block trades, derived blocks and Basis Trade at Index Close transactions**
