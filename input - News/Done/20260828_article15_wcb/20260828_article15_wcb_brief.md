- **[A]** The combined firm would rank **fourth** in the industry, behind **Citic Securities, Guotai Haitong Securities and Huatai Securities**
- **[A]** Done through **stock swaps** with the two firms' shareholders · the Shanghai exchange approved a **trading suspension of up to 25 days** · approvals still needed from **company boards, shareholders and regulators**
- **[B]** The **Shanghai Stock Exchange has accepted the application**, per a report dated **16 June 2026**
- **[B]** A preliminary share-swap agreement was signed on **19 November**, with the detailed scheme released a month later
- **[B]** CICC would issue **more than 3.1 billion mainland shares at 36.68 yuan (US$5.42) each**
- **[B]** Swap prices: **Dongxing at 16.05 yuan · Cinda at 19.11 yuan** · ratios of **1 to 0.4376** and **1 to 0.5210**
- **[B]** **Central Huijin Investment** controls all three — as of 31 December it held **40%** of CICC directly, **45%** of Dongxing and **79%** of Cinda indirectly
- **[B]** The merged company assumes all **assets, liabilities, businesses, employees, contracts, qualifications, rights and obligations** of both targets
- **[B] Results:** CICC operating revenue **28.5 billion yuan (US$4.2 billion)**, net profit **9.8 billion yuan (US$1.4 billion)** · Dongxing **4.7 / 2.1 billion yuan** · Cinda **4 / 1.9 billion yuan**
- **[B] Assets under management, 31 December:** CICC **782.8 billion yuan (US$115.8 billion)** · Dongxing **114.2 billion yuan** · Cinda **130 billion yuan**
- **[B] Shares that day:** CICC **+0.7% at 33.93 yuan** in Shanghai · Dongxing **+0.5% at 12.93 yuan** · Cinda **+0.1% at 15.48 yuan**
- **[A] CICC statement:** *"The restructuring is conducive to building a first-class investment bank and supporting the reform of the financial market and the high-quality development of the securities industry."*
