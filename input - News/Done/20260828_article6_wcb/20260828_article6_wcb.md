# IC Enters Esports With Multi-Year BLAST Deal Spanning Counter-Strike and Dota 2

A broker best known for its Formula 1 branding has moved to the edge of a Counter-Strike stage. BLAST announced on **14 July 2026** in Copenhagen that **IC**, one of the world's largest online trading providers, is the **Official Online Trading Partner of BLAST Premier and BLAST Slam** under a multi-year agreement that marks the trading firm's entry into esports.

## What the deal is

The company described the term only as **multi-year** and put no value on it. What the release does spell out is scope: IC holds the trading-partner title across both BLAST Premier and BLAST Slam, and the deal carries the brand into BLAST's **international Counter-Strike and Dota 2 ecosystems**.

## Where IC will actually appear

The activation runs through **live broadcasts, arena events, original content and online experiences via BLAST.tv**. BLAST said IC would be woven across the fan experience through broadcast branding, arena visibility, bespoke content, fan competitions and on-site activations, and that the audience reached amounts to millions of highly engaged, digitally native fans. The release offers no figures behind that claim.

## BLAST Higher Lower, the trading hook

One element sits closer to IC's day job than a logo ever could. IC will present **BLAST Higher Lower**, BLAST.tv's Counter-Strike 2 skin value game — a skin being a tradable cosmetic weapon item players buy and sell inside the game. According to the release, the format turns on **value, price comparison and quick judgment**, which the company calls key factors in online trading. IC will also meet fans and guests at selected BLAST arena events through physical activations.

## Building on the Formula 1 portfolio

The esports move extends a sponsorship strategy IC already runs in motorsport, where it backs the **Toyota Gazoo Racing Haas F1 Team**. The release ties both deals to the same three words: precision, performance and technology.

James Brown, Commercial Director at BLAST, said IC "is exactly the type of ambitious global brand that shows where esports is heading," adding that the partnership "goes far beyond brand visibility" because IC becomes part of the broadcast, the arena experience, the content and the way fans engage with BLAST.tv.

Tony Philip, Chief Marketing Officer at IC, said BLAST "brings together technology, performance, precision and highly engaged global communities, making esports a natural extension of our sponsorship strategy." He framed the deal as a route to a new generation of digitally native audiences.

## The debut stage and who the two sides are

BLAST named **BLAST Premier Bounty** in Malta, held from **25 July to 3 August 2026**, as IC's first appearance. The tournament opens BLAST's second competitive Counter-Strike season of 2026, with **32 teams** selecting their opponents directly and competing for a **$1,150,000 USD** prize pool.

BLAST calls itself a global competitive entertainment company, with offices in Copenhagen, London, Berlin, Mumbai, Malta and New York City and work spanning publishers including Riot Games, Epic Games, Valve, Supercell and Ubisoft. IC, **founded in 2007 in Australia**, offers trading in Forex, indices, commodities, stocks, bonds and cryptocurrencies and says traders in **over 200 countries** use it. The company states it ranks No. 1 globally for retail FX and other volumes, citing the Finance Magnates Q4 Report.

The stage lights, for once, are pointed at the order book.
