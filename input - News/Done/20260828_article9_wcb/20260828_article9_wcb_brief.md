## 📊 Key Facts (from raw data — do not add figures)
- **Intercontinental Exchange, Inc. (NYSE: ICE)** announced on **29 June 2026** (dateline LONDON & NEW YORK, via BUSINESS WIRE) the planned launch of **its first economic indicator futures contracts**
- Two families: **global monetary policy decisions** and **US natural gas storage reports**
- Central banks referenced: **the U.S. Federal Reserve System, the European Central Bank and the Bank of England** — the release calls them *"the three most systemically important central banks in the world"* (ICE's wording)
- Gas contracts track **US natural gas storage inventory levels, published weekly by the U.S. Energy Information Administration**
- Contract mechanics: **cash-settled**, **exchange-traded and centrally-cleared**, designed to let market participants express views on specific economic events and decisions
- **Launch planned for 10 August 2026**, described as *planned launch* and *subject to regulatory completion*
- **Ten product codes:** OID; OIS; OIR; EUD; EUS; EUR; MPL; MPS; MPR; EWP
- **Quote — Trabue Bland, Senior Vice President of Futures Markets at ICE:** *"ICE's expansion into economic indicator contracts reflects demand for regulated onshore products that allow customers to take positions on economically relevant risks that shape markets."* He adds the products leverage ICE's global trading and clearing platform, offering *"a new approach to hedging significant moments impacting global markets."*
- The contracts follow **ICE's Polymarket Signals and Sentiment** service, a prediction data and analytics offering giving professional and institutional traders normalized feeds of Polymarket's crowd-sourced probability assessments
- ICE is a **Fortune 500** company operating futures, equity and options exchanges **including the New York Stock Exchange**, plus clearing houses
